## Findings

### 1. Legal-Claim Risk

**Finding:** Needs work before production publication.

- “Strategic Results” can be read as a promise or expectation of a favorable result. It is acceptable as a clearly labeled development headline, but a safer production alternative is “Strategic Perspective” or “Strategic Legal Analysis.”
- “Specialized legal research,” “high-level legal research,” “expert legal analysis,” and “specialist support” describe capability and should be substantiated by the service owner’s actual qualifications, scope, and engagement model.
- “Investor protection,” “business and human rights,” “environmental responsibility,” and “regulatory risks” are broad subject descriptors, not guarantees; service pages must retain explicit scope, exclusions, and jurisdiction limits.
- “Complex legal questions require specialized expertise” is positioning language. It should not imply that the platform is the only or uniquely qualified provider.
- “Africa-focused” and “internationally connected” are positioning claims. Confirm they accurately describe the operating model and evidence.

### 2. Credential and Identity Risk

**Finding:** Blocker pending verification.

- “International law scholar,” “legal researcher,” and “dispute-resolution specialist” are credential or authority claims.
- Verify the person’s preferred name styling, biography, academic or professional basis for each descriptor, and whether any regulated title may be inferred.
- Do not add degrees, admissions, bar memberships, institutional affiliations, rankings, publications, appointments, or case history unless supplied and verified.
- Do not imply that the profile establishes authorization to provide legal services in any jurisdiction.
- Keep “Learn More About Tezzeta” as a link from the homepage to the About profile. Do not use that label for a contact-page link, which could misstate the destination.

### 3. Jurisdiction and Service-Scope Risk

**Finding:** Blocker pending approval.

- The service list spans international arbitration, investment law, trade, extractive industries, business and human rights, and cross-border business.
- Confirm which jurisdictions are authorized, which users may be served, and whether the platform provides legal services, research support, academic consultancy, or a combination.
- Keep service records fixture-marked and production-unpublished until the service model, engagement process, conflicts/eligibility, fees, cancellations, and support rules are approved.
- Preserve the existing wording that a consultation does not by itself establish an attorney-client or equivalent professional relationship.

### 4. Translation Risk

**Finding:** Blocker pending qualified review.

- The French and Chinese versions below are conservative working translations, not certified legal translations.
- Review terminology for “international investment law,” “investor-State disputes,” “choice of law,” “jurisdiction,” “business and human rights,” “AfCFTA,” “environmental responsibility,” and “dispute resolution.”
- Decide whether Chinese means Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, or both, and define target regions.
- Confirm that translated disclaimers preserve the same limits as the English source and do not strengthen service or credential claims.
- Do not publish meaning-sensitive translations until a qualified reviewer approves them.
