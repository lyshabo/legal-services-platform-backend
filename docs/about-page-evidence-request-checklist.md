## Purpose

Use this checklist to collect, review, and approve the evidence required for each
identity, credential, experience, image, and translation statement on the About page.
No item is approved merely because it appears in a CV, draft website, public search
result, or prior publication.

## Decision Rule

- Mark an item **Approved** only when the named responsible reviewer has examined the
  evidence, confirmed the exact public wording, and recorded a sign-off date.
- Use only these status values: **Not supplied**, **Under review**, **Approved**,
  **Rejected**, or **Expired**.
- A rejected, expired, incomplete, or conflicting item keeps the related website claim
  blocked.
- Do not infer identity matches, current professional status, jurisdictional authority,
  institutional relationships, client relationships, results, image rights, or
  translation approval.
- The name `Tezzeta Mbuya N'Gungwa` must be authoritatively linked to the CV name
  `Tezzeta Mbuya` before CV-sourced claims are approved under the published name.

## 10. Stop Conditions

Production publication must remain blocked when any of the following is true:

- The published name is not authoritatively linked to the CV identity.
- Current bar status, professional authorization, or jurisdiction remains unverified.
- A regulated title is used without locale- and jurisdiction-specific approval.
- A degree, current doctoral status, institutional role, or date range lacks acceptable evidence.
- Experience wording could imply an undisclosed client, representation, outcome, or
  institutional endorsement.
- Portrait likeness, copyright, release, crop, or alt text is not approved.
- The consultation workflow, pre-engagement disclaimer, or service jurisdictions are not approved.
- French, Simplified Chinese, or Traditional Chinese lacks its own named qualified reviewer,
  target region, terminology decisions, and dated sign-off.
- Evidence has expired, conflicts with another record, or cannot be reproduced by the reviewer.
- The website copy differs materially from the version the reviewer approved.
