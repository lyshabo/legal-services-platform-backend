  Information flow.
- The selected language is passed into the guidance evaluator and exposed in
  the rendered result metadata.
- The English copy is the source track; French, Simplified Chinese, and
  Traditional Chinese remain working translations pending qualified review.
- No locale may convert “preliminary information” into legal advice, an
  individualized conclusion, or a promise of professional representation.
- Chinese script tracks are separate. Traditional Chinese is additive and does
  not replace Simplified Chinese.
