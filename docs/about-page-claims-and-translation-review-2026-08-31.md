# About Page Claims and Qualified-Translation Review

**Review date:** August 31, 2026
**Locales:** English, French, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese
**Publication status:** Gated. Not cleared for unrestricted production publication.
**Scope:** Marketing-claim substantiation, professional-credential risk, semantic equivalence, terminology, and qualified-review requirements.

## Review Standard

This is an operational content review, not a jurisdiction-specific legal opinion.
Objective and implied claims should have a reasonable evidentiary basis before
publication. Communications about legal services should not contain material
misrepresentations or omit information needed to prevent the communication from
being materially misleading.

Primary reference points:

- FTC Policy Statement Regarding Advertising Substantiation:
  https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/ftc-policy-statement-regarding-advertising-substantiation
- ABA Model Rule 7.1, Communications Concerning a Lawyer's Services:
  https://www.americanbar.org/groups/professional_responsibility/publications/model_rules_of_professional_conduct/rule_7_1_communication_concerning_a_lawyer_s_services/
- SRA Code of Conduct, paragraphs 8.6-8.11 on client information and publicity:
  https://guidance.sra.org.uk/solicitors/standards-regulations/code-conduct-solicitors

The governing rules for the Kinshasa/Gombe Bar Association and every target
jurisdiction must be confirmed by the responsible legal reviewer before
production publication.

## Claim-by-Claim Review

### 1. "International Law and Dispute Resolution Specialist"

- **Type:** Professional-competence and potentially regulated-title claim.
- **Call:** Terminology and jurisdiction review required.
- **Evidence needed:** Current professional-status record; documented work,
  research, publications, or instruction across the stated fields; confirmation
  whether "specialist" is restricted or certification-implying in each target
  jurisdiction.
- **Safer fallback:** "International law and dispute-resolution professional"
  or "Focused on international law and dispute resolution."
- **Gate:** Legal and jurisdiction reviewer.

### 2. "Mbuya works across international investment law, arbitration,
natural-resource governance, and Africa-focused legal and economic questions."

- **Type:** Objective experience and subject-matter claim.
- **Call:** Retain with controlled evidence.
- **Evidence needed:** Research records, engagement descriptions, publications,
  conference materials, or work-product index covering each named field.
- **Risk:** "Works across" is broad but does not promise outcomes or claim
  exclusive expertise.

### 3. "Qualified member of the Kinshasa/Gombe Bar Association"

- **Type:** Current professional credential.
- **Call:** Resolved in the project record; retain only while the authenticated
  evidence and approval record remain current.
- **Evidence needed:** The authenticated record already reviewed, identity
  match, effective date, jurisdiction, evidence location, and approving owner.
- **Gate:** Reopens if the record expires, status changes, or the wording is
  used in a jurisdiction requiring additional disclosure.

### 4. "Independent counsel"

- **Type:** Current professional-status and service-capacity claim.
- **Call:** Scope qualification required.
- **Evidence needed:** Current engagement structure, authorized practice scope,
  jurisdiction, entity or trading-name details, and professional-insurance or
  regulatory information where applicable.
- **Risk:** May imply availability to provide legal representation in every
  visitor's jurisdiction.
- **Required safeguard:** Preserve jurisdiction, engagement, and
  non-relationship disclaimers.

### 5. "Doctoral researcher in investment arbitration"

- **Type:** Current academic-status claim.
- **Call:** Substantiation required.
- **Evidence needed:** Current University of Essex enrollment or official
  researcher-status confirmation, program title, and current dates.
- **Suggested precision:** Use the institution's official status label exactly.

### 6. "More than ten years" of legal practice and advisory experience

- **Type:** Quantified experience claim.
- **Call:** Substantiation required.
- **Evidence needed:** Dated role history demonstrating at least ten years of
  qualifying legal practice and advisory work without double counting.
- **Risk:** The surrounding project has previously used "15 years." Select one
  evidence-supported convention and apply it consistently.

### 7. "Advised governments"

- **Type:** Client-category claim.
- **Call:** Substantiation required; heightened confidentiality review.
- **Evidence needed:** Redacted engagement letter, public mandate, reference,
  invoice record, or client-approved description identifying the government
  category without exposing privileged or confidential information.
- **Gate:** Client identity and publication permission.

### 8. "Advised investors"

- **Type:** Client-category claim.

- **Gate:** Confidentiality and publication permission.

### 9. "Advised mining companies"

- **Type:** Client and sector claim.
- **Call:** Substantiation required.
- **Evidence needed:** Redacted engagement evidence and approval for public use.

### 10. "Advised international organizations"

## Qualified-Translation Review

### Cross-Locale Findings

- The four versions preserve the same five-paragraph narrative sequence and do
  not add claims absent from English.
- The full identity heading remains `Tezzeta Mbuya N'Gungwa`; prose uses
  `Mbuya`.
- Automated tests and screenshots confirm UTF-8 rendering, locale persistence,
  and layout. They do not establish qualified legal translation approval.
- Named qualified reviewers, qualification basis, target regions, terminology
  decisions, and actual sign-off dates have not been supplied. All translation
  approvals remain open.

### French

**Status:** Meaning substantially aligned; qualified review required.

Required decisions:

1. Replace `conseil indépendante` with `conseil juridique indépendante` unless
   the responsible jurisdictional reviewer approves a regulated professional
   title such as `avocate indépendante`.
2. Consider `à l'Université d'Essex` instead of `à l'University of Essex` for
   natural French, while retaining the institution's official identity.
3. Confirm whether `entreprises et droits humains` or the more established
   legal term `entreprises et droits de l'homme` is preferred for the target
   region and institutional audience.
4. Confirm `africanisation` against the official research title.
5. Confirm whether `Barreau de Kinshasa/Gombe` is the approved public form.

### Simplified Chinese

**Status:** Meaning aligned; Mainland China or specified target-region legal
review required.

Required decisions:

1. `合资格会员` is understandable but may read as a direct translation.
   Consider `具备执业资格的会员` or the official translated status.
2. Confirm the approved translation of `Kinshasa/Gombe Bar Association` and
   whether `律师协会` accurately describes the institution.
3. Replace `基础法律教育` with the verified degree name where evidence permits;
   it is currently cautious but imprecise.
4. Confirm `投资者与国家争端解决机制的非洲化` against the official research
   terminology.
5. Confirm whether `专业人士` is preferred over any regulated or
   certification-implying term.

### Traditional Chinese

**Status:** Meaning aligned; target region must be specified before approval.

Required decisions:

1. `合資格會員` is natural in some Traditional Chinese regions but the official
   institutional-status translation must control.
2. Confirm whether the target region prefers `律師公會` or `律師協會`.
3. Confirm `諮詢`, `顧問`, `爭端`, and `爭議` terminology for the intended
   Hong Kong, Taiwan, or other regional audience.
4. Replace `基礎法律教育` with the verified degree name where approved.
5. Confirm `投資者與國家爭端解決機制的非洲化` against the official research
   title and regional legal usage.

## Publication Recommendation

Keep the current publication gate closed. The copy is structurally suitable for
a controlled development or temporary demo, but unrestricted production
publication requires:

1. A claim-evidence matrix with stable evidence locations and permission status.
2. Jurisdictional review of `specialist`, `independent counsel`, Bar wording,
   current authorization, and service availability.
3. Confidentiality-safe support for every client-category and matter claim.
4. Current academic and institutional records.
5. Named qualified translation reviewers, defined target regions, terminology
   decisions, and dated sign-off for French, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional
   Chinese.
