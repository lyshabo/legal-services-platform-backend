   research, advisory support, and doctoral research concerning investment

   commercial arrangements, regulation, and international economic relations.

   legal-instrument work associated with UNCITRAL, research and case support at
   Leigh Day, and independent research and advisory work involving commercial
   contracts, public-private partnerships, mining, regulatory matters, and
   cross-border disputes. Publication and conference records remain subject to
   their separate evidence and permission controls.
