# DRC Legal Library Source Map

**Prepared:** September 14, 2026  
**Input:** `The Democratic Republic of the Congo Laws 2.docx`  
**Purpose:** Map the remaining proposed Legal Library instruments to official or authenticated primary sources while preserving fail-closed publication controls.

## Status Key

- **Source verified:** An official or authenticated primary-source page identifies the instrument and its date.
- **Consolidation pending:** The source identifies the instrument, but a current consolidated text, amendment list, or effective-date history still requires legal review.
- **Permission pending:** No explicit licence or republication permission for copying the text into this portal has been established.
- **Fixture only:** The portal may show a metadata record, source link, and neutral description; it must not publish the text, sell a copy, or imply current legal completeness.

## Publication-Gate Decisions

1. **No exact text publication yet.** A source link or index entry is not evidence of a current consolidated text, complete amendment history, or permission to reproduce.
2. **No purchase or download controls.** All mapped resources remain development fixtures with disabled purchase/download actions.
3. **No current-law claim.** Public summaries must use “source identified,” “development index,” or “subject to currentness review,” not “current law” or “complete code.”
4. **Separate legal and translation review.** Each resource requires title, issuing body, instrument type, adoption/publication/effective date, currency status, and permission status in every locale.
5. **Treaty-specific verification.** BIT and REC categories must be decomposed into individual treaty records before any claim about DRC participation, force, amendment, or termination.

## Remaining Owner Actions

- **DRC legal reviewer:** obtain official gazette PDFs or authenticated consolidated texts for the banking/credit, anti-corruption, CENAREF, Criminal Code, land/property, tax, and REC items.
- **Currentness reviewer:** confirm later amendments, repeals, implementing regulations, and effective dates as of the publication date.
- **Rights reviewer:** confirm whether each source may be copied, excerpted, translated, indexed, or linked, and record the governing licence or written permission.
- **Treaty reviewer:** verify DRC signature, ratification/accession, entry into force, reservations, protocols, and termination status for every BIT and REC instrument.
- **Translation reviewers:** approve French, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese titles, summaries, limitations, and jurisdictional caveats independently.
