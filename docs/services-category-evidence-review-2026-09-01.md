# Services Category Evidence Review

**Reviewed:** September 1, 2026
**Asset:** Services catalog and individual service-detail routes
**Publication status:** Development-only; all service records remain fixture records, evidence-pending, and subject to professional, jurisdictional, and qualified-translation approval.

## Scope

The catalog contains 14 individual service records. Each record now has differentiated four-locale copy for:

- Who it is for
- What is included
- What is not included

Each record also carries:

- `evidenceStatus: "pending"`
- a jurisdiction-specific review note
- source IDs and APA 7-style reference metadata
- a localized publication/professional-review status notice

The browser-only static demo mirrors this structure without adding authentication, booking, payments, provider calls, or confidential submissions.

## Cross-Category Review

- Scope language is differentiated by service and does not rely on a single generic “consultation” statement.
- “Included” fields describe research, issue mapping, source review, or pre-engagement analysis rather than guaranteed professional outcomes.
- “Not included” fields expressly address representation, filing, litigation, negotiation, deadlines, expert appointment/admissibility, regulatory approval, certification, investment advice, and outcome limits where relevant.
- Jurisdiction-specific examples are labeled as examples and are not presented as universal rules.
- Evidence about a legal concept is not treated as evidence of professional qualification, authorization, current service availability, or client outcomes.
- French, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese text is synchronized in structure and scope but remains gated for qualified translation approval.
- No service record has been promoted to production, and booking remains disabled for the newly requested categories.
